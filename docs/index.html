A practical overview of the tools included in the School Management System project.

Students & academics

◷

School operations

Staff, library, inventory, transport, hostel, health records, events, and visitor management.

▣

Fees & finance

♡

Communication

Announcements, notifications, parent-teacher meetings, messaging, and leave requests.

▥

Learning tools

Learning content, course modules, learning paths, discussion forums, and teaching progress.

◎

Reports & insights

Role-based dashboards, analytics, audit logs, bulk operations, and academic-year transitions.
